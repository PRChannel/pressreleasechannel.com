What you buy

Managed outreach against a custom-built list

PR Fire's differentiator is the custom list: rather than selling fixed packages, it builds a media list for the story and manages outreach to the journalists and editors on it. For UK and US campaigns, this is a genuine middle path between self-service platforms and full retainers.

China is where the custom-list method meets its structural limit — and the reason is worth understanding rather than just noting. Chinese media publication is not primarily won by pitching journalists. It runs through editorial submission processes, and in practice through established placement channels with the outlets. A well-built list of Chinese contacts, however accurate, does not substitute for that infrastructure. This distinction is explained in what overseas brands should ask before paying.

What to verify

Two checks for China buyers

  1. Ask how the China portion would actually be executed. If the answer is a list plus outreach, ask for published China URLs from previous campaigns as evidence the method works there.
  2. Separate the two products if you need both. PR Fire's outreach for Western media and a China placement service are different purchases with different verification — see local China distribution.
When this model fits

Strong for Western story-led outreach, limited for China

For a newsworthy story aimed at UK or US journalists, custom-list outreach is often the most efficient spend available. For China, the honest position is that the method itself — not the provider's quality — is the constraint.

Can PR Fire build a Chinese media list and pitch Chinese journalists?

It offers international targeting, but list-based pitching is structurally weak in China, where publication depends on editorial submission processes and established placement channels rather than journalist pitches.

When should a buyer choose custom outreach over placement services?

When the story is genuinely newsworthy for Western media and earned coverage is the goal. When verifiable placement on Chinese media is the goal, a local execution service is the appropriate product.
