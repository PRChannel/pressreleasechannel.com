What you buy

Distribution circuits built for corporate and financial news

Business Wire is structured around circuits: combinations of geographic and industry distribution sold at quoted prices. It is widely used for earnings, M&A announcements and material corporate news, and its strength is that context — disclosure-grade infrastructure and reach into financial and business media.

China is available within this circuit model. What a circuit does not include is media-by-media selection: the buyer does not pick named Chinese outlets from a rate card before submission. The China footprint that results is therefore determined by the circuit composition and downstream pickup, not by a pre-agreed outlet list.

What to verify

Three checks for the China portion of a Business Wire purchase

  1. Get the China line item in writing. Ask which China-specific distribution the quote covers — a regional circuit is not the same as localized Chinese-language distribution to Chinese media websites.
  2. Classify the resulting pages. Distinguish network-controlled pages, syndicated press release pages and genuine editorial placements before counting coverage.
  3. Verify domains and durability. Open every China-visible URL, check the domain against the outlet's official presence, and keep dated copies.

These checks apply the framework in our guides on distribution infrastructure versus individual media placement and pickups versus editorial placements.

When this model fits

Best for material corporate news, thinnest for local China visibility

Business Wire is a strong choice when the announcement is financial or material and the priority is disclosure-grade, multi-market distribution with one workflow. It is a weaker match when the goal is verifiable coverage on named Chinese media websites that a Chinese audience can find and an overseas buyer can audit.

If the second goal matters, compare this model with local China press release distribution and China distribution pricing, or return to the full provider directory.

Does Business Wire publish on Chinese media websites?

Business Wire distributes to its network and syndication channels, which can include China-relevant circuits. Publication on specific Chinese media websites depends on the circuit and downstream pickup, so it should be verified from the actual URLs in the distribution report rather than assumed from the purchase.

Is Business Wire or a local China service the better choice?

They are different products. Business Wire fits disclosure-grade multi-market distribution; a local China service fits buyers who need pre-selected Chinese outlets, verified publishing URLs and page-type classification. The right question is which outcome the announcement actually needs.
