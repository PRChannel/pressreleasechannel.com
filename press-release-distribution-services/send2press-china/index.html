Send2Press for China

Send2Press is a pay-per-release US newswire with targeted editor outreach and syndication; it is not a China-focused channel, and its results should not be counted as Chinese media coverage.

What you buy

Per-release distribution with an editor-outreach component

Send2Press sells individual releases at accessible prices, combining network syndication with targeted outreach to editors by plan. The model is straightforward and the entry price is low, which makes it a reasonable tool for US-market announcements.

The China boundary is unambiguous: the service is not China-focused and has no local China execution. For a buyer building a China communication plan, Send2Press can be part of the English-language global layer, but it cannot be the China layer, and no combination of its plans changes that.

What to verify

One check for China buyers — and one warning

  1. Do not count its syndication as China coverage. Any China-visible pages from a Send2Press release will be syndicated or aggregator pages, not editorial placements on Chinese media websites.
  2. Watch for resellers mislabeling it. If a third party sells a package claiming Send2Press-style distribution "including China," ask exactly which Chinese domains will host the result. This is the same test described in fake media placement and media list scams.
When this model fits

Right tool for the US layer, wrong tool for the China layer

Use Send2Press when the announcement targets US trade and news media at a controlled cost. When the same announcement needs China coverage, plan a separate China-specific product rather than extending this one.

Can Send2Press distribute my press release to Chinese media?

No. Send2Press is a US-focused distribution service and is not China-focused. Its syndication may produce internationally visible pages, but these are not Chinese media placements.

Should I use Send2Press and a China service together?

You can. They cover different markets and do not overlap: Send2Press for the US/English layer, a local China service for Chinese media placement. The key is treating them as separate purchases with separate verification.
