What you buy

Packaged distribution with volume pricing

NewswireJet's structure is straightforward: tiered packages by distribution breadth, with discounts for multiple releases. The economics reward companies planning a series of announcements — the per-release cost drops as volume rises.

Its China status is listed as "not clearly China-focused," which is itself useful information. When a provider's China capability is unclear rather than confirmed absent, the verification burden shifts to the buyer: ask for China-delivered URLs from recent campaigns before assuming anything in either direction.

What to verify

Three checks given the unclear China status

  1. Ask for evidence, not assurances. Request live URLs from recent distributions that produced China-visible results. If none exist, the answer is clear.
  2. Classify whatever is produced. Any China-visible pages should be typed and domain-checked per the verification method.
  3. Do not let volume discounts blur scope. A cheaper per-release price on a global package is not progress toward China coverage; the two are separate line items.
When this model fits

Sensible for serial global announcements, unproven for China

For companies with a steady cadence of English-language announcements, the volume pricing is coherent. For China, the honest status is "unverified" — treat it as absent until shown otherwise.
