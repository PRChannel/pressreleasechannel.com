WebWire for China

WebWire sells products from direct web posting up through wire and targeted media distribution; its global reach is distribution framing, and it does not provide local China media execution.

What you buy

A product ladder from posting to targeted media

WebWire's range is wider than many self-service platforms: the base product is a public posting on its own site, with paid options extending to wire distribution and a targeted media option. This makes it a midpoint between pure self-publishing and full newswire service.

For China, the structure is familiar: "global reach" describes where content can circulate, not where editorial relationships exist. The targeted media option points at English-language media lists. None of the products in the ladder connect to the Chinese publishing environment, where placement runs on Chinese platforms through local editorial and placement infrastructure.

What to verify

Two checks for China buyers

  1. Scope the targeted media option before assuming reach. Ask which media the targeting covers and in which language — the answer defines the product's actual market.
  2. Keep China out of the WebWire line item. Its results are global-layer distribution; the China column needs a China-specific provider. The planning logic is in what you are actually paying for.

Does WebWire distribute to Chinese media outlets?

No. WebWire is not China-focused; its global reach refers to distribution circulation. It does not execute locally in China or publish on Chinese media websites.

Is the targeted media option worth it for a China announcement?

Not for China itself. The targeting covers English-language media lists. A China announcement needs a China execution product, planned and verified separately.
