What you buy

Tiered self-service distribution at a low entry price

IssueWire's model is accessibility: submit the release yourself, choose a tier, and receive distribution across online channels with a pickup report. For a small company wanting an announcement to exist publicly somewhere beyond its own website, the model does that at minimal cost.

The China boundary is the same as for other US self-service platforms, and it is worth being blunt about: low price and low China relevance usually travel together. Cheap distribution produces cheap page types — platform postings, syndicated entries, aggregator listings. None of these are editorial placements on Chinese media websites.

What to verify

Two checks for China buyers

  1. Read the pickup report as page types, not counts. "Picked up by 100 sites" is meaningless until each site is classified — the method in why pickups are not placements.
  2. Recognize the padding pattern. Platforms in this category are the bulk material of inflated media lists. If a package shows you a large count built from sites like this, compare it against the scam patterns before paying.

Does IssueWire distribute to Chinese media outlets?

No. IssueWire is a US-based self-service platform and is not China-focused. Its pickup report reflects online distribution, not Chinese media publication.

Why is cheap distribution a warning sign for China buyers?

Because genuine Chinese media placement requires local editorial and placement infrastructure that has a real cost. Prices far below that level are usually achieved by counting free or near-free platform pages as "placements."
