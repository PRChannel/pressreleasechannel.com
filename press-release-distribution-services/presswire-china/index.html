What you buy

Distribution plus a media database to target it with

Presswire's structure gives buyers two connected tools: distribution for individual releases and a media database for targeting decisions. Tracking and online syndication round out the package. This is a flexible setup for communications teams that want to control who receives their news.

For China, the database model raises a specific question: a list of Chinese contacts is not the same as working relationships with Chinese newsrooms. Chinese media publication typically runs through editorial submission processes and, in practice, established placement channels — which is why global targeting tools tend to underperform local execution in China.

What to verify

Three checks on any China-targeted Presswire campaign

  1. Test the targeting assumption. Ask which Chinese outlets in the database have accepted recent international releases, and request evidence — published URLs, not contact counts.
  2. Verify language handling. Confirm whether the Chinese version of the release is professionally adapted for Chinese media conventions, per preparing a release for Chinese media.
  3. Audit the delivered URLs. Classify page types and verify domains against the outlets' official presence.
When this model fits

Good toolkit for controlled outreach, limited by China's media structure

For teams running their own targeted outreach in Western markets, Presswire's distribution-plus-database model is coherent. For China, the same model meets an environment where contact lists matter less than editorial and placement infrastructure.

Can Presswire's media database reach Chinese journalists?

It offers global targeting options, but a contact list is not a placement channel. Chinese media publication depends on editorial acceptance and, commonly, established placement infrastructure — which is what local China services provide.

What is the strongest question to ask before buying?

Ask for published URLs from recent China-targeted distributions. Evidence of outcomes is the only reliable substitute for assumptions about a database.
