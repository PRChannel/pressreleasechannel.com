What you buy

A free public page, with paid exposure upgrades

The base product at 1888PressRelease is a free public posting on the platform; paid tiers add featured placement and wider distribution. Like other self-publishing platforms, it creates an indexable public record without involving any third-party newsroom.

For a China buyer the boundary holds at every tier: the publishing environment is the platform itself, in English, outside the Chinese media ecosystem. Paid upgrades increase visibility inside that environment; they do not cross the boundary into Chinese media publication.

What to verify

Two checks for China buyers

  1. Label free-platform pages correctly in any report. A public page is a public page, whatever tier produced it. The labeling discipline is in what a coverage report should show.
  2. Trace where "free platform" pages come from in packages. Platforms like this are standard filler in inflated media lists — see the five patterns in fake media placement and media list scams.
When this model fits

Zero-cost record for zero-budget announcements

For announcements that need a public record and have no budget, the free tier is honest value. It should appear in reporting as what it is — a self-published page — and never as media coverage.

Can 1888PressRelease publish my release on Chinese media?

No. It is a self-publishing platform that posts releases on its own English-language site. Chinese media publication requires local China execution, which it does not provide.

Is the free tier worth using at all?

As a free public record, yes — with honest labeling. As a component of China coverage, no, under any tier.
