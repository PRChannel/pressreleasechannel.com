What you buy

One-time packages, no subscription

The News Guy's model is transactional: single-release packages at entry prices, with higher tiers adding broader international distribution and reporting. There is no subscription or platform to learn — a buyer pays per release and receives network distribution.

The China boundary follows the tier structure. The international tier extends where the release circulates, but circulation is a distribution concept. Chinese media publication requires local submission infrastructure that no tier in a US-based package model includes. If a reseller represents an international package as "covering China," ask for the specific Chinese domains in advance — the test from the fake placement guide.

What to verify

Two checks for China buyers

  1. Define what the international tier delivers. Distribution breadth and China coverage are different purchases; get the deliverable list in writing.
  2. Verify and classify any China-visible pages. Use the page-type method from how to verify a placement before you pay before counting anything.
When this model fits

Simple one-off global distribution

For occasional announcements needing straightforward network distribution without a platform subscription, the one-time model is practical. China remains a separate product.

Does The News Guy's international package cover Chinese media?

No. The international tier broadens distribution reach but does not include local China execution or publication on Chinese media websites.

What is the main risk with package-based international tiers?

Ambiguity: "international" can be read as covering specific countries when it only means wider circulation. China buyers should ask for the deliverable list, not the tier name.
