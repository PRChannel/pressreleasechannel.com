What to verify

Four checks before buying from any unverified provider

  1. Confirm the entity. Who operates the site, from where, with what registration? An operating entity that cannot be identified is a disqualifying finding.
  2. Request evidence of past China deliveries. Live URLs from recent China-targeted campaigns — opened by you, not screenshotted by them. The evidence standard is in how to verify a placement before you pay.
  3. Test the claim set against known fraud patterns. Fixed placement counts at very low prices, vague outlet names and screenshot-only reporting are the patterns described in the fake placement guide.
  4. Pay small and verifiable first. Whatever the eventual relationship, the first purchase should be the smallest one that still produces checkable URLs.
