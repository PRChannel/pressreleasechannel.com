What you buy

A distribution guarantee — scoped to participating outlets

PRLeap guarantees distribution to its participating outlets and provides a publicity report. The guarantee language is worth reading precisely: it covers distribution into the network's participating outlets, which is a defined and deliverable promise.

For China buyers, the same precision applies to the boundary. The participating-outlet network is a US-oriented, English-language set. A guarantee is only as valuable as the specificity of what it names — and this is actually a useful lesson for evaluating any provider, including on the China side, where the equivalent promise should name outlets, page types and verifiable URLs. That standard is described in what you are actually paying for.

What to verify

Two checks for China buyers

  1. Do not extend the guarantee to China by assumption. The guarantee covers participating outlets; no Chinese media websites sit inside that set.
  2. Use the guarantee standard as your benchmark elsewhere. When a China provider promises results, hold the promise to the same specificity: which outlets, which page types, which verifiable URLs — the framework in placement verification.

Does PRLeap guarantee publication on Chinese media websites?

No. Its guarantee covers distribution to participating outlets in its own network, which is US-oriented and not China-focused.

What makes a guarantee meaningful?

Specificity: named outlets, defined page types and verifiable URLs. A guarantee that names none of these reduces to distribution into the provider's own environment.
