PR Underground for China

PR Underground sells budget tiered distribution with syndication and pickup reporting; it is a US self-service platform with no China execution, and its reports should be read as page types, not placement counts.

What you buy

Basic and national tiers with pickup reporting

PR Underground structures its product in two simple tiers — basic online distribution and a national tier with wider syndication — and reports pickups afterward. The workflow is fully self-service, and the price point targets small businesses and startups.

For China planning, the relevant fact is what the pickup report will and will not contain. It will contain syndicated pages and platform postings on English-language channels. It will not contain articles published by Chinese media organizations, because the service has no connection to the Chinese publishing environment.

What to verify

Two checks for China buyers

  1. Classify before counting. Apply the page-type breakdown from what a coverage report should show to any report that mixes platform pages with claimed coverage.
  2. Price China honestly. A China layer built on verified local placement costs more than budget self-service, and the difference is the cost of actual editorial infrastructure. The economics are laid out in why China distribution costs what it costs.
When this model fits

Startup budget, global layer only

PR Underground is a defensible choice for the basic global-visibility slot on a small budget. The China slot needs a China-specific provider.

Can PR Underground get my release onto Chinese media websites?

No. It is a US self-service platform with no China execution. Any China-visible pages would be incidental syndication, not Chinese media placements.

Is its pickup report useful?

Useful for what it measures — where the release appeared online — provided each URL is classified by page type before being counted as coverage.
