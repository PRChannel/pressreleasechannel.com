What you buy

Multi-country reach with regional targeting

GlobeNewswire distributes releases across a large international network with regional, industry and financial targeting options. China is reachable through its Asia-Pacific regional targeting, and the service is commonly used for earnings, corporate announcements and multi-market product news distributed in one workflow.

The model is syndication-driven: the release is published through the network and made available to downstream outlets and aggregators, with pickup beyond the network depending on relevance and outlet behavior. For China specifically, this means the distribution is real, but the shape of the resulting China footprint — which domains, which page types, how durable — is determined after the fact rather than selected before submission.

What to verify

Four checks for the China portion of a GlobeNewswire report

  1. Which China-visible pages resulted? Request live URLs for everything that appeared in China, not a country-level distribution confirmation.
  2. What environments host them? Distinguish established media websites from aggregators, syndicated press release pages and self-publishing environments before counting coverage.
  3. Are the pages publicly accessible and durable? Open each URL, check the domain against the outlet's official presence, and keep dated copies.
  4. Does the language version match the audience? A release distributed into an Asia-Pacific circuit in English produces a different footprint than localized Chinese distribution — confirm which one you bought.

The reasoning behind these checks is covered in our guide to verifying placements before you pay and our article on why pickups are not the same as editorial placements.

When this model fits

Efficient for one-to-many global news, thinner for China-specific coverage

GlobeNewswire is a strong choice when one announcement needs to reach many markets at once with consistent formatting and reporting. It is a weaker match when the goal is selected, verifiable coverage on named Chinese media websites that a buyer or Chinese audience can audit later.

If the second goal matters, compare this model with local China press release distribution, our China distribution pricing, and the full provider directory before deciding.

Does GlobeNewswire distribute releases to Chinese media?

GlobeNewswire offers regional targeting that includes Asia-Pacific and China, with distribution through its network and syndication channels. The China-specific results should be confirmed from the delivered URLs, since local Chinese media placement is not the primary model.

Can GlobeNewswire handle a Chinese-language release?

Language capabilities depend on the product configuration. Buyers targeting Chinese readers should confirm in writing whether the release is distributed in Simplified Chinese, professionally adapted, and which Chinese channels receive it.
