What you buy

A promised number of placements, at a price that invites questions

King Newswire's packages advertise fixed placement counts at very low prices. The economics deserve a direct statement: real editorial placement on established media carries a real cost, so a fixed count of "placements" at a few dollars each is achieved through page types that cost little or nothing to produce — platform pages, syndicated entries, aggregator listings.

This does not automatically make the service fraudulent. It makes the deliverable a category question: placements of what, on which domains, reviewed by whom? The same question, asked of any China package, is the core of the fake placement and media list scam guide.

What to verify

Three checks before any fixed-placement purchase

  1. Get the list before paying. Named outlets with real domains, not outlet counts.
  2. Classify every promised page type. Main-site article, syndicated page, content account or self-publishing platform — each has a different value, and only the first resembles editorial coverage.
  3. Archive at delivery. Save dated copies of every URL so the evidence outlives the report. The method is in how to verify a placement before you pay.
When this model fits

Only with the page types priced honestly

If a buyer wants low-cost online visibility and accepts that the "placements" are low-authority pages, the purchase can be rational. The failure mode is buying the count while imagining editorial coverage.

Are King Newswire's fixed-placement packages legitimate?

They deliver pages, and pages are real. The legitimacy question is about classification: if the "placements" are platform and syndication pages sold as editorial coverage, the buyer has been misled regardless of whether URLs were delivered.

What is the single most useful habit when evaluating packages like this?

Divide the price by the placement count and ask what kind of editorial process that number could plausibly fund. If the answer is "none," you now know what page types to expect — verify them.
