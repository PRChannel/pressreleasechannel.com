What you buy

Managed service with writing support, aimed at US media

eReleases occupies a middle position between self-service platforms and full global newswires: a buyer pays a fixed price per release and receives managed distribution, release writing support and industry-based targeting, with distribution powered by an underlying newswire network.

The orientation is US media. An international add-on extends distribution beyond the US, but this extension is syndication-level reach, not local execution in the target country. For China specifically, that distinction matters more than usual, because the Chinese publishing environment is structured differently from Western media markets and rewards local handling.

What to verify

Three checks before treating the international add-on as China coverage

  1. Ask what the add-on physically produces in China. Distribution into syndication channels and pages on Chinese media websites are different deliverables; request the exact list.
  2. Check the language question. Confirm whether any China-visible result is published in Chinese and adapted for Chinese readers, or only the English release syndicated.
  3. Verify and archive the URLs. Classify page types and keep dated copies of every China-visible result.

The language and page-type issues are covered in what international PR teams misunderstand about Chinese media.

When this model fits

Strong for US announcements with PR support, not a China product

eReleases fits companies announcing to US media that want writing help and managed handling at a known price. For a China-facing announcement, the honest reading of the product is that China is an adjacent add-on, not a capability.

If China coverage is the actual goal, compare local China distribution, China pricing, and the rest of the directory.

Can eReleases get my press release published on Chinese media websites?

eReleases is a US-focused managed service; China is available only as an international add-on with syndication-level reach. Verifiable publication on named Chinese media websites is not the product's core deliverable and should not be assumed.

What is the difference between an international add-on and local China execution?

An international add-on extends distribution reach beyond the home market. Local execution means in-country media selection, Chinese-language handling, editorial submission and verified publishing URLs. The add-on is cheaper and easier; the local execution produces auditable China coverage.
