What you buy

A tier ladder from posting to distribution

24-7 Press Release makes the product ladder unusually legible: the lowest tier is essentially a public posting on the platform, and higher tiers add wider distribution. This transparency is useful — it lets a buyer see exactly how much distribution a given price buys.

What the ladder does not contain, at any tier, is China. The platform is US-oriented and English-language throughout. For a China buyer, the useful mental model is the same as for other self-service platforms: this is the global English layer, and every tier on it stops at the same China boundary.

What to verify

Two checks for China buyers

  1. Match the tier to the real goal. If the goal is a public record, the low tier may suffice; paying for a higher tier will not move the result closer to Chinese media, because the boundary is categorical, not a matter of spend.
  2. Keep verification standards uniform. Whether a page comes from a $29 tier or a $399 one, the classification rules in how to verify a placement apply identically.
