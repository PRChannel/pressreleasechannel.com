What to verify

Four checks, starting with the provider itself

  1. Confirm current China delivery specifics with the provider. Ask which Chinese channels are included, in what language, and with what page types — in writing.
  2. Get a sample report. A real distribution report from a recent China-targeted campaign is the fastest way to see what the product produces.
  3. Verify the sample the way you would verify a purchase. Open the URLs, check the domains against the outlets' official presence, classify the page types.
  4. Confirm pricing and permanence terms before comparing the quote against other providers.

This process follows the directory's own method, described in how we evaluate Chinese media placements.
