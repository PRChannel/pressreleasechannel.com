What you buy

A newswire network with package-based targeting

PR Newswire is one of the largest global newswire networks, with regional distribution options that include Asia-Pacific and China targeting. Purchases are generally structured around membership, plans and package-based circuits rather than per-outlet selection. China is available as a distribution target, but the buyer is buying access to a distribution circuit — not picking named Chinese media websites from a rate card before submission.

This means the report a buyer receives will primarily document distribution across the network's channels and syndication paths. How much of that report translates into visible, durable pages on established Chinese media websites depends on the specific product configuration — which is exactly the detail an overseas buyer should pin down before purchase.

What to verify

Five questions for any China-inclusive package

  1. Which China outcomes are guaranteed? Distribution, publication on network-controlled sites, and named-outlet placement are different deliverables.
  2. What will the China portion of the report contain? Ask for a sample report and check whether it lists live URLs or only outlet names and counts.
  3. What page types result? Main-site news articles, syndicated pages and content-account publication should be distinguished, not merged into a headline count.
  4. Are the domains the outlets' real domains? Verify each against the media organization's official presence.
  5. How does pricing work for China specifically? Market-based pricing can vary significantly by circuit and language; get the China line item explicitly.
