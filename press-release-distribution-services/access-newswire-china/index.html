What you buy

Distribution plus software, with international markets priced on request

ACCESS Newswire's structure has two layers: pay-per-release distribution and subscription software for ongoing PR workflows. North American distribution is the core product; other markets, including China, are handled through quoted international packages.

Because China is a quoted add-on rather than a standard circuit, the scope of "China distribution" can vary between quotes. That makes the pre-purchase conversation unusually important: the buyer should establish exactly which Chinese channels the quote covers before comparing prices across providers.

What to verify

Three checks for a quoted China package

  1. Ask for the China deliverable list. A quote should specify which China-relevant channels, platforms or syndication paths are included — in writing, before acceptance.
  2. Separate analytics from placements. Distribution analytics measure activity; media coverage is measured by verifiable published URLs. Do not let the former substitute for the latter.
  3. Verify the delivered pages. Open each China-visible URL, classify its page type, and check the domain against the outlet's official presence.

For the underlying classification logic, see why pickups are not the same as editorial placements and what you are actually paying for.

When this model fits

Efficient for workflow-driven teams, undefined for China until quoted

ACCESS Newswire suits organizations running regular PR workflows that want distribution plus tooling from one provider. For a one-off China announcement that needs verifiable local coverage, the quoted model adds a scope risk that the buyer must manage through explicit questions.

Can ACCESS Newswire distribute a press release in China?

International markets including China are available by quote, but local China media execution is not the provider's primary model. The China scope of any specific quote should be confirmed in writing and verified from delivered URLs.

Why is quote-based China distribution riskier than a fixed product?

Because the deliverable is defined during the sales conversation rather than by a published product page. Anything not written into the quote — channels, page types, permanence — is not reliably part of the purchase.
