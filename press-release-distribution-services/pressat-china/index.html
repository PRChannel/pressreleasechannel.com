What you buy

UK media distribution in two pricing modes

Pressat serves the UK market with both one-off releases and subscription plans, distributing to syndication channels, media subscribers and analytics-tracked pickups. Its strength is UK media accessibility at mid-market prices.

"International reach" in its packaging describes where syndicated content can appear, not local placement capability in foreign markets. For China, this means the service can produce technically visible pages but cannot produce what Chinese media coverage actually requires: local media selection, Chinese-language handling and editorial submission inside the Chinese publishing environment.

What to verify

One boundary, one warning

  1. Treat any China-visible syndication page as ancillary. It is not a placement and should not appear in a coverage count as one. The counting logic is in why 200 pickups may not mean 200 placements.
  2. Question any "China included" claim from a reseller. If a package promises UK-style distribution "plus China," demand the specific Chinese domains in advance — the test from the media list scam guide.
When this model fits

UK announcements, yes; China announcements, no

Pressat is a sensible choice for UK-market announcements on a pay-as-you-go basis. China goals need a different product, planned separately.

Does Pressat distribute press releases in China?

No. Pressat is UK-focused; its international reach refers to syndication visibility, not China media placement. China-visible syndicated pages are not Chinese media coverage.

What is the difference between international reach and local execution?

International reach means content can appear in more places through syndication. Local execution means someone in the target market selects outlets, submits to editorial processes, and delivers verifiable URLs. Only the second produces auditable media coverage.
