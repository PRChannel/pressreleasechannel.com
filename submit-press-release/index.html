Submit a Press Release

Send us the release first. You do not need to choose a plan yet.

PR Channel can review your announcement, company information and China media objectives, then tell you whether free public information access, targeted media distribution or a custom campaign is the more appropriate next step.

What to send

Three things are enough to start

01
Your press release

Word, PDF, Google Doc link or plain text is fine for the initial review.

02
Your official company website

This helps us verify the source organization and understand the company context.

03
Your China media goal

Tell us if you simply want public information available in China, need industry media, want specific outlets, or need media invitation support.

No extra authorization page is required for the free plan.
The submitter only needs to confirm that they are authorized to provide the announcement to PR Channel for distribution. PR Channel may add a distribution note to the published version.

Review boundaries

What we do not accept for China Media Access

The free option exists so that a real corporate announcement can be read in China. It is not a backlink service and it is not a translation desk. The submissions below are declined without a review cycle — this list is published so you do not spend time preparing material we will not use.

Owned content presented as news

A blog post, brand story, founder letter or "we have published an article" notice is not an event. If the only new fact is that you wrote something, there is nothing to report.

Old coverage restated with a new date

A 2021 report republished in 2026 under a new headline. We date-check every claim against its original source and will cite the earlier publication if it exists.

Submissions that name the backlink as the goal

If the request asks for "resulting URLs", domain authority, link placement or SEO value, the answer is no. We publish announcements; we do not sell links.

No China or cross-border relevance

A domestic service business with no China customers, operations, partners or product is not a China media story — however true and however well documented the story is.

Generated text with no responsible human

If no named person can answer a factual question about the release, it is declined. A template sender address is not a contact.

Requests for us to write or translate

The announcement and its language version are the submitter's responsibility, the same way the original release is. We do not draft releases and we do not translate them into Chinese.

Claims that cannot be traced to a source

We check the company registration, the official website and every link in the release. A claim that cannot be traced to a document, a public record or a named source does not get published.

Anything that would make us vouch for the company

We publish the announcement. We do not endorse the business, its pricing, its service quality or its warranty terms, and we do not remove the distinction between what a company states and what a third party has verified.

A decline is not a permanent ban. A company turned down for a blog post is welcome to submit again when it has a real development — a new product, a distribution agreement, a contract award, an export order or a new location. What we will not do is publish the same material with a new date attached to it.
