<section class="section"><div class="container"><div class="content"><p>This framework is used to explain media placements more clearly to overseas companies buying press release distribution in China. It is not a ranking system and it does not claim that one page type is always better than another. The purpose is to identify what the buyer is actually receiving.</p></div>
<div class="principle-list">
<div class="principle"><h3>1. Actual publishing domain</h3><p>We check where the article is actually hosted. A media brand name and a publishing domain are related, but they are not always the same thing.</p></div>
<div class="principle"><h3>2. Page type</h3><p>Main-site news pages, regional channels, content accounts, special-topic pages, app environments and other publishing formats should not be treated as interchangeable.</p></div>
<div class="principle"><h3>3. Public accessibility</h3><p>Where possible, a placement should be independently accessible through a public URL rather than supported only by a screenshot or report entry.</p></div>
<div class="principle"><h3>4. Media relevance</h3><p>Industry, geography, audience and announcement type matter. A recognizable media name is not automatically relevant to every company announcement.</p></div>
<div class="principle"><h3>5. Editorial review</h3><p>Submission is not the same as publication. Editors or media outlets may accept, edit, delay or reject a release.</p></div>
<div class="principle"><h3>6. Publication vs recommendation</h3><p>A live article does not automatically mean homepage recommendation, app recommendation or algorithmic promotion. These should be described separately.</p></div>
<div class="principle"><h3>7. Verification after publication</h3><p>After publication, the final URL, domain, date and page type should be checked so the buyer can understand the delivered result.</p></div>
<div class="principle"><h3>8. Page persistence</h3><p>Published pages may change or disappear over time. Where a resource has an explicit support period, that period applies; otherwise PR Channel's default support period is one month.</p></div>
<div class="principle"><h3>9. Search visibility is separate</h3><p>A published page may or may not be indexed or ranked by Google, Baidu or another search engine.</p></div>
<div class="principle"><h3>10. AI visibility is separate</h3><p>Publication does not guarantee AI retrieval, citation, recommendation or inclusion in an AI-generated answer.</p></div>
</div>
<div class="content" style="margin-top:40px"><h2>Why this matters for overseas buyers</h2><p>Global distribution reports can be useful, but buyers still need to understand the local page-level result. This framework is designed to make the final placement easier to compare, verify and explain internally.</p><p><a href="/china-media-guide/media-placement-verification/">Browse media placement and verification guides →</a></p></div></div></section>
