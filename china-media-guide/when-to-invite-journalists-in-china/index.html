When Should a Company Invite Journalists to an Event in China?

When media invitation is useful in China, how it differs from press release distribution, and why journalist attendance does not guarantee coverage.

China PRMedia VerificationOverseas Brands

For companies organizing an event in China, one question often comes up: should we invite journalists, or is online press release distribution enough?

The answer depends on whether the event itself can create something worth reporting.

Invite journalists when the event creates new information

Product launches, major partnerships, research announcements, factory openings, industry forums and executive interviews can all create meaningful reporting opportunities when there is genuinely new information.

Invite journalists when there is something worth interviewing

One of the main advantages of attendance is the possibility of producing information that does not already exist in the prepared release.

On-site value matters

Exhibitions, demonstrations, site visits and open days can be easier to understand in person than through a written announcement alone.

Attendance does not guarantee coverage

A journalist may attend and still decide not to publish. Coverage depends on news value, editorial judgment, audience relevance, competing news and the quality of the available information.

Media invitation and distribution are not substitutes

Distribution is useful when the company already has a clear piece of information. Invitation is useful when the event can create additional reporting material through interviews and observation.

More journalists do not automatically mean better coverage

A smaller group of relevant journalists may be more useful than a large group with little connection to the topic.

A simple decision test

  1. Is there a real news development?
  2. Is there something journalists can observe on-site?
  3. Are there people worth interviewing?
  4. Is the event relevant to a specific media audience?
  5. Can journalists obtain information beyond the prepared release?
The better question is not “How many journalists can we invite?” It is “Is there enough news value for journalists to come?”
