A global newswire can be the right tool for many campaigns. But not every China press release requires a global distribution system.

Sometimes a local China PR partner can be more practical.

## Use a local partner when China is the main market

If the campaign is focused only on China, the company may not need global distribution infrastructure, multi-country coordination, international circuit selection or worldwide reporting.

A local provider can focus the entire campaign on Chinese media selection and execution.

## Use a local partner when page-level detail matters

Some buyers want more than a media list. They want to know the actual publishing domain, page type, media relevance, public accessibility and how the placement can be verified.

A local provider may be better positioned to discuss these details because it works with the local publishing environment every day.

## Use a local partner when the media mix needs to be flexible

Global newswires often organize distribution through standard circuits and packages. That is efficient at scale.

But some campaigns need a more customized mix: a regional expansion, niche industrial announcement, local exhibition, targeted B2B story or China-only product update.

## Use a local partner when localization requires judgment

A China-ready release may need more than translation. It may require decisions about headline style, local context, claims, terminology, company background and supporting facts.

## Use a local partner when you want media invitation or journalist coordination

For product launches, exhibitions, forums, signing ceremonies, interviews or site visits, a company may need on-site media invitation or journalist coordination.

A local provider can often combine distribution with local event support more naturally than a global newswire product.

## Use a global newswire when international consistency matters more

A global provider can be more suitable when one announcement must be distributed worldwide, procurement requires a globally approved vendor, headquarters wants one reporting format across regions or several language markets need to launch simultaneously.

## Some companies should use both

A multinational company might use a global wire for international disclosure and broad distribution, while a local China partner manages selected Chinese media, verification and event-related execution.

## The decision should start with the campaign objective

Ask:

- Is China the only target market?
- Do we need original journalist engagement or only distribution?
- Do we need local event support?
- Is detailed media verification important?
- Does headquarters require a global vendor?
- How much customization do we need?

## Final thought

A local China PR partner makes more sense when the campaign depends on local detail, flexible media selection and direct execution.

A global newswire makes more sense when the campaign depends on scale, standardization and international coordination.

Neither model is universally better. The right choice depends on what the company actually needs to accomplish.
