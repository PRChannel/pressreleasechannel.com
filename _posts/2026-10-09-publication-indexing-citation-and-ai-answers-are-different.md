A media article being published is only the first stage of public visibility.

For companies evaluating SEO, GEO or AI visibility, four outcomes should be separated: publication, indexing, citation and final-answer inclusion.

## Publication

Publication means a public page exists. This can usually be verified through the live URL, domain and page type.

## Indexing or retrieval

Search engines and AI systems decide independently whether to discover, index or retrieve that page. Publication does not guarantee this step.

## Citation

Even when a system retrieves a page, it may not cite that source in a visible answer.

## Final-answer inclusion

A brand can be present in a source without being mentioned in the final answer. AI systems may combine, summarize or omit information based on the question and the available evidence.

For practical GEO work, it is more useful to track these stages separately than to treat “published” as equivalent to “visible in AI.”
