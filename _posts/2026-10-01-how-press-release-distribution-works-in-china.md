For overseas companies, press release distribution in China can look simple from the outside: prepare an announcement, choose media and publish it.

In practice, the process has several layers. The media name, the page type, the review process and the final public URL can all affect what the placement actually means.

## Start with the news, not the media list

A useful distribution plan begins with the announcement itself. What happened? Who needs to know? Is the information relevant nationally, regionally or within a specific industry?

Media selection should follow the subject and audience. Choosing outlets only because the names are recognizable can produce a list that looks strong but is poorly matched to the story.

## Understand the publishing environment

A familiar media brand may appear through different publishing environments. A main-site news page, a regional page, a content account and a special-topic page should not automatically be treated as the same type of placement.

This is why the actual domain and page type matter.

## Submission still involves editorial review

Sending a press release does not mean publication is automatic. Editors or media outlets may accept, edit, delay or reject a submission.

The more factual and clear the announcement is, the easier it is to assess whether a media placement is appropriate.

## Verify the live page after publication

Once an article is live, check the public URL. Useful verification points include the domain, page type, accessibility and whether the page can be opened independently.

A screenshot can document a moment in time, but it should not replace the live page when a public URL exists.

## Publication, indexing and AI visibility are different

A published article may or may not be indexed by Google or other search engines. It may or may not be retrieved, cited or used by an AI system.

These are separate stages:

1. publication;
2. search indexing or retrieval;
3. citation or recommendation;
4. inclusion in a final answer.

Treating all four as one outcome creates unrealistic expectations.

## A practical workflow

For an overseas company, a reasonable process is:

1. confirm the factual announcement;
2. define the audience and geography;
3. select relevant media types;
4. understand the expected page type;
5. submit for editorial review;
6. verify the live URL after publication;
7. evaluate search or AI visibility separately.

Press release distribution works best when the company understands what is being published, where it is being published and what the placement can realistically achieve.
