How the $0 plan works

No extra website post is required

The company submits a legitimate press release and confirms that it is authorized to provide the material to PR Channel for distribution in China.

PR Channel may add a short authorization note to the published version. The company does not need to create a separate announcement or backlink on its own website.

Authorization note shown with the release

Distribution note: [Company Name] has authorized PR Channel, a China-based media communications service, to distribute this announcement for media communication purposes in China.

The note is used only after the submitter confirms that they are authorized to provide the announcement for distribution.

Important

What the free plan does not include

China Media Access is designed as an entry point for verified public information, not as a substitute for paid media placement.

  • No guaranteed publication on specific Chinese media outlets.
  • No paid media resources are included.
  • No guaranteed editorial coverage.
  • No guarantee of search engine indexing or AI visibility.
  • PR Channel may decline content that does not meet publication standards.
  • Free access may be limited by company, submission frequency and available publishing capacity.

FAQ

Before you choose a plan

Does the free plan require my company to publish a separate authorization page?

No. The submitter only needs to confirm that they are authorized to provide the press release to PR Channel for distribution. PR Channel may display a short distribution note with the published release.

Does PR Channel guarantee publication on specific media outlets?

No. Specific media publication remains subject to the requirements and editorial review of each outlet.

Can I request specific Chinese media?

Specific outlet requests are better handled through China Targeted or a Custom China Campaign, depending on the media and campaign requirements.

Do all published URLs have the same value?

No. PR Channel evaluates the actual domain, page type, accessibility, relevance and publishing environment rather than treating every URL as an equivalent media placement.
