faq:
  - q: "When does media invitation make sense in China?"
    a: "Media invitation is most useful when an event creates new information, visual context or meaningful interview opportunities — for example product launches, industry forums, exhibitions, signing ceremonies, factory visits, corporate open days and executive interviews."
  - q: "Does journalist attendance guarantee media coverage?"
    a: "No. Journalist attendance does not guarantee publication. Editorial decisions remain with the journalist or media outlet, and coverage angle, timing and final publication may differ from the company's expectations."
  - q: "What does PR Channel coordinate for media invitation?"
    a: "Depending on the event, coordination may include identifying relevant media, communicating event information, confirming attendance, arranging interview opportunities and helping the company prepare clear background materials."
  - q: "How is media invitation different from press release distribution?"
    a: "Press release distribution starts with prepared company news submitted for editorial review and publication. Media invitation is used when an event may justify journalist attendance, interviews or on-site reporting."
