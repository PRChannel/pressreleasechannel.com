<section class="section"><div class="container content"><h2>When media invitation makes sense</h2><p>Media invitation is most useful when an event creates new information, visual context or meaningful interview opportunities. Common scenarios include product launches, industry forums, exhibitions, signing ceremonies, factory visits, corporate open days and executive interviews.</p><h2>What PR Channel coordinates</h2><p>Depending on the event, coordination may include identifying relevant media, communicating event information, confirming attendance, arranging interview opportunities and helping the company prepare clear background materials.</p><h2>What it does not guarantee</h2><p>Journalist attendance does not guarantee publication. Editorial decisions remain with the journalist or media outlet. Coverage angle, timing and final publication may differ from the company's expectations.</p><p><a href="/china-media-guide/press-release-distribution-vs-media-invitation-in-china/">Read the practical guide: Press Release Distribution vs Media Invitation in China →</a></p></div></section>
