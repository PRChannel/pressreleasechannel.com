About PR Channel

A China-based media communications service

PR Channel helps companies distribute press releases, match relevant media and coordinate journalist attendance in China.

What PR Channel is

PR Channel is operated by Jingmen Anmao Network Technology Co., Ltd. The company was founded in 2019, and PR Channel has been operating since 2021.

Our work is centered on press release distribution in China, with additional support for media invitation, journalist coordination and practical public-information planning.

How we approach media work

We prefer clear descriptions of media resources over broad promotional claims. A recognizable media name should not replace verification of the actual domain, page type and public URL.

We also separate publication from later visibility. A live article does not automatically mean search indexing, ranking, AI retrieval or citation.
