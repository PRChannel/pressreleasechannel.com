      <h2>Primary service: Press Release Distribution in China</h2>

      <p>PR Channel primarily supports overseas companies that need to distribute factual business announcements through relevant Chinese media. The work can include media matching, editorial submission, page-type clarification and verification of the final published URL.</p>

      <p>PR Channel focuses on helping companies understand not only whether content was published, but also where it appeared, what type of page was used and how the placement can be verified.</p>

      <h2>Related services</h2>

      <p>PR Channel also supports media invitation, journalist coordination and media placement verification when those services are relevant to the communication objective.</p>

      <h2>How we approach Chinese media</h2>

      <p>We emphasize the actual publishing domain, page type, public accessibility, media relevance and realistic editorial boundaries.</p>

      <p>Publication, recommendation, search indexing and AI citation are treated as separate outcomes. A published page does not automatically mean search indexing, recommendation by a platform or inclusion in an AI-generated answer.</p>

      <p><a href="/how-we-evaluate-chinese-media-placements/">Read how PR Channel evaluates Chinese media placements →</a></p>

      <h2>Official websites</h2>

      <p>PR Channel maintains separate web resources for international and China-facing audiences.</p>

      <p><strong>International website:</strong><br>
      <a href="https://pressreleasechannel.com/">https://pressreleasechannel.com/</a></p>

      <p>This English-language website provides information for overseas companies about press release distribution in China, Chinese media placements, media invitation, placement verification and related communication practices.</p>

      <p><strong>Official China website:</strong><br>
      <a href="https://www.epr.ink/">https://www.epr.ink/</a></p>

      <p>The China-facing website provides Chinese-language information about PR Channel services, media resources, press release distribution, media invitation and related media communication services in China.</p>

      <p>Both websites are associated with PR Channel and are operated by Jingmen Anmao Network Technology Co., Ltd.</p>
