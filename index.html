How we judge placements

Media names are not enough

We evaluate the actual page and publishing context behind a placement, not the logo alone.

Actual domain

Check where the page is really hosted and whether the domain matches the claimed media environment.

Page type

Main-site news, content accounts, regional pages and recommendation placements are not equivalent.

Public accessibility

A placement should be independently accessible and verifiable, rather than supported only by screenshots.

Editorial boundaries

Publication, recommendation, indexing and AI citation are separate outcomes and should not be presented as the same thing.

Read our media placement principles →

Service boundaries

What we do not promise

Clear boundaries are part of responsible media communications.

No guaranteed publication

Media outlets retain editorial control over acceptance, editing, timing and publication.

No permanent-page guarantee

Published pages may change or disappear over time. Where a resource has an explicit support period, that period applies; otherwise the default support period is one month.

No guaranteed search or AI inclusion

Publication does not automatically mean indexing, ranking, citation, recommendation or inclusion in an AI-generated answer.
