What press release distribution means in practice

In China, a recognizable media name does not always describe the exact page a company will receive. A placement may appear on a main-site news page, a regional channel, a content account, a mobile page or another publishing environment associated with the media brand.

For overseas companies, the useful question is therefore not only “Which media name is included?” but also “What kind of page will be published, where will it be accessible, and how can it be verified?”

Suitable types of announcements

Common use cases include product launches, corporate updates, research cooperation, regional expansion, industry events, new projects, executive viewpoints and other business developments with a clear factual basis.

Editorial boundary: Media submission does not guarantee publication. Final editing, acceptance and timing depend on the outlet or editor.

How to evaluate a media plan

A stronger plan normally considers relevance before volume. A smaller set of suitable media can be more useful than a large list of unrelated outlets. We recommend checking the media domain, page type, public accessibility and whether the placement description can be independently verified.

Publication is not the same as visibility

A live article can exist without ranking for a target keyword. It can also exist without being cited by an AI system. Search indexing, ranking, retrieval and AI citation are separate outcomes and should be measured separately.
